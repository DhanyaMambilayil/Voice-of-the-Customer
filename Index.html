Metric definitions

CSAT — average of Happy, Respect, Speed and Visit-Again scores, converted from a 1–5 scale to a 0–100% index.
Trust Score — average of Respect and Visit-Again scores, converted to a 0–100% index.
Top-box rate — % of respondents who gave the maximum score (5/5) on the Happy question.
NPS — Al Ansari — computed from the native 0–10 recommend question: Promoter 9–10, Passive 7–8, Detractor 0–6. NPS = %Promoter − %Detractor.
NPS — BEC — BEC's survey uses a 1–5 recommend question, not 0–10. By agreed convention: 5 = Promoter, 3–4 = Passive, 1–2 = Detractor. Because this is a coarser scale than Al Ansari's, cross-brand NPS comparisons should be read directionally, not as exactly equivalent instruments.
Comment rate — % of respondents who left any text in the Improvement field.
Response share — a branch's responses as a % of its brand's total responses.
Top / bottom branches — ranked by NPS; branches with fewer than 5 responses are excluded from ranking, since a handful of ratings can swing NPS by ±50 or more.
Anomaly detection — flags (a) the same phone number rating the same branch more than once within 30 minutes, and (b) rows with malformed, placeholder, or missing Civil ID/Mobile values. These are statistical red flags for further review, not confirmed fraud.
